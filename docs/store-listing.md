## Full Description

### English
Family Recipe Box helps you keep your family's favorite dishes in one tidy, beautiful place. Add a recipe, write down the steps and the tips that matter, and find it again the moment you need it — all on your device.

**Features:**
- Clean card-based recipe grid
- Western categories: Main, Side, Salad, Dessert — long press any category to rename it
- Drag recipe cards to reorder them the way you cook
- Random recipe picker — roll the dice and stop wondering what to make
- Add your own photos to any dish, or keep the built-in illustrations
- Back up and restore all your recipes (photos included) at any time — always free, and the easy way to move to a new phone
- Works fully offline. No account, no sign-in, and nothing is ever uploaded
- Multi-language support: English, Chinese, Japanese, German, French

**Free version:**
- Save up to 20 recipes

**Full version — one-time purchase:**
- Unlimited recipes, yours forever
- No subscription, no recurring charges
- One payment, all future updates included

Payments are processed securely by Google Play. This is a one-time purchase, not a subscription.

## Screenshots Required

Google Play requires at least 2 phone screenshots per locale. Recommended: 8 screenshots showing:

1. Home grid with recipes
2. Category filtering (Main / Side / Salad / Dessert)
3. Random recipe picker dice
4. Edit recipe screen
5. Recipe detail with a user photo
6. Settings screen
7. Recipe limit dialog — shows the 20-recipe free limit and the one-time unlock
8. Language picker

Recommended resolution: 1080x1920 or 1920x1080 (phone).

> 第 7 张是有意放进去的：免费版上限是这类应用最主要的差评来源，
> 把说明型弹窗提前展示出来，可以让用户在上架前就预期到这个限制，而不是装完才被拦住。
