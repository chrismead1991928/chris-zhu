Privacy Policy

Family Recipe Box
Effective date: October 8, 2026
Last updated: October 8, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how chris zhu ("we", "us") handles information in the Family Recipe Box mobile application (the "App").

Summary: we do not collect your data. The App has no account system and no servers of our own. Your recipes, photos and settings are stored only on your device. We have no way to read them, and nothing is ever uploaded to us.

1. Information We Collect

We collect no personal information. The App does not ask you to create an account and does not transmit your recipe content anywhere.

1.1 Recipe content you create

Recipes you add — titles, categories, ratings, favourite marks, cooking steps, tips and the photos you attach — are stored in a private database inside the App on your device. When you attach a photo, the App immediately copies that image into its own private storage, so the recipe image keeps working even if you later delete or move the original in your gallery. This content is never transmitted to us or to any third party. It leaves your device only in the following cases, both of which you initiate yourself:

  • You use the App's backup function, which writes a single archive file to a location you choose on your own device. The App does not upload it anywhere — whether that file lands in local storage, on a USB drive, or in a cloud folder you have configured is entirely your decision, made through the system file picker.
  • You use the share function and pick a destination app (for example a messaging app).

1.2 Purchase information

The optional full version unlock is sold and processed by Google Play Billing as a one-time purchase. We do not receive or store your name, email address or payment card details. The App stores a single local flag indicating whether a purchase was detected, so that unlocked features work correctly while offline. Your purchase history is held by Google, not by us.

1.3 Diagnostic information

If the App fails to start or hits an unexpected error, it writes a technical diagnostic record (an error type and a technical stack trace) to a private file on your device. This record is stored locally and is never sent to us automatically. It may be shown to you inside the App, and it is shared with anyone only if you deliberately tap a share button.

1.4 Information we do not collect

The App does not request or collect any of the following:

  • Account credentials, email address, display name or any account identifier
  • Precise or approximate location
  • Contacts, calendar or call logs
  • Camera or microphone access (photos are chosen from your gallery on your own initiative, and a chosen photo is copied into the App's private storage rather than read from your gallery on an ongoing basis)
  • Advertising identifiers, or any advertising or behavioural analytics data
  • Device files outside the images you deliberately select

The App's own manifest declares exactly one permission, android.permission.INTERNET. The Google Play Billing library that the App includes merges in a small number of additional permissions of its own — the billing permission and network-state access it needs to talk to the Play Store. Those come from the library, not from us, and the App itself asks for nothing further. The App issues no network requests of its own: there is no sync, no analytics and no remote content loading. The App does not request storage, location, camera or contacts permissions.

Sharing a recipe as an image and creating a backup both work through Android's system file and share mechanisms. The App therefore needs no storage permission: you choose the destination yourself, and the App is granted access only to the specific file you picked.

2. How We Use Information

We do not use your information, because we never receive it. Every operation below happens entirely on your device.

PurposeWhere it happens
Storing and displaying your recipe collectionEntirely on your device
Unlocking full version featuresOn your device, based on a local purchase flag from Google Play
Diagnosing crashes you choose to reportOn your device, and shared only if you tap share

3. Where Your Data Is Stored

All App data lives in the App's private storage area on your device, protected by Android's application sandbox. Other apps cannot read it, and we cannot access it.

Because there is no server, uninstalling the App or clearing its data in Android settings permanently removes everything. There is no copy anywhere else.

4. Sharing and Third Parties

We do not sell, rent or trade your information, and we have none to share. The only third party involved is:

  • Google, for Google Play Billing and because the App is distributed through Google Play. Google's handling of your account and purchase data is governed by Google's Privacy Policy.

The App contains no advertising networks, no analytics SDKs, no social plugins and no data brokers. No recipe text or image is ever sent to an external service.

5. Data Retention

Your recipe data is retained on your device until you delete it in the App, uninstall the App, or clear application data in Android settings. We hold no copy and therefore have no retention period of our own.

6. Your Rights and Choices

6.1 Your data

  • Delete individual recipes inside the App.
  • Back up your recipes at any time — creating and restoring a backup is free and always available, including on the free version. Restoring a backup never counts against the free version limit, so your data can always come back.
  • Uninstall the App, or clear its data from Android → Settings → Apps, to remove all local data.

6.2 Access, correction and portability

You can view, edit and back up all of your content directly in the App. There is no server-side data for us to retrieve, correct or delete on your behalf.

6.3 Regional rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights under laws such as the GDPR or the CCPA, including rights of access, correction, deletion, portability and objection. Because we do not collect or process your personal data, we are generally not able to act on such requests — there is nothing held on our side. Questions are still welcome at the address in section 10.

7. Security

Local data benefits from Android's application sandboxing and, on modern devices, from full-disk encryption. Because no data is transmitted to us, there is no server-side database to breach. Please keep your device and your Google account credentials secure.

8. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your jurisdiction). Because the App collects no personal information at all, it does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Material changes will be reflected in an App update. Continued use of the App after changes take effect constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
